[ about ]

A lil’ about me.

Terrance Lamonte, Jr. is a Chicago-based actor, host, and teaching artist. A non-union performer with international experience, Terrance has graced stages and screens across Chicago and Amsterdam, performing as an ensemble member with the globally recognized Boom Chicago and serving as house ensemble on multiple mainstage productions at The Second City Chicago. His commercial work spans continents, with campaigns for Coke Zero and Casetify produced out of the Netherlands. Television credits include PBS-WTTW’s The Great Chicago Quiz Show.

  • On stage

    Sketch, improv and character work, sharpened as an ensemble member at Boom Chicago and The Second City.

  • On the mic

    Emcee and host for conferences, keynotes and awards, prepared enough to make it look unprepared.

  • In the room

    Workshops and coaching for artists, ensembles and corporate teams that build trust, communication and play.
