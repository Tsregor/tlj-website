A lil’ about me.

I grew up on the West Side of Chicago, where I learned early that comedy is everywhere; you just have to know where to look. I’ve spent the past decade turning that into a career. Stage-wise: The Second City’s Mainstage and National Touring Company, ComedySportz Chicago, Laugh Out Loud, Mission IMPROVable, and a move overseas to join Boom Chicago’s Mainstage cast in Amsterdam. Screen-wise: WTTW, VICE, and commercial campaigns for Coke Zero and Casetify. Comedy isn’t just a stage thing for me. It’s how I teach, host and build community, which is a fancy way of saying I help people think on their feet, collaborate better and maybe even enjoy their next meeting.

  • On stage

    Sketch, improv and character work. Reps logged at Boom Chicago and The Second City.

  • On the mic

    Emcee and host for conferences, keynotes and awards. I over-prepare so it looks like I didn’t.

  • In the room

    Workshops and coaching for artists, ensembles and corporate teams. Trust, communication and play. No trust falls required.
